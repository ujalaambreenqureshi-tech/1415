Jumma Couture by Noman accepts direct manual payments via Bank Transfer, Easypaisa, and JazzCash. Cash on Delivery (COD) is not offered by our business. Every order is verified by our team before crafting and dispatch.

3. Do you ship internationally? Which countries are supported?

Yes, we ship worldwide via DHL Express and FedEx International Priority to patrons across Pakistan, United States, United Kingdom, Canada, United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Australia, Germany, France, and across the European Union.

4. How do color swatches work when selecting an outfit?

When you click any circular color dot beneath a product, the actual corresponding product image changes instantaneously to display that specific color. The selected color variant and SKU are automatically recorded in your shopping bag and synced with Shopify.

5. How can I track my order once paid?

Immediately following 100% advance payment verification, you receive an official order confirmation with a unique reference number (e.g. HRT-2026-XXXX). You can monitor your garment's progress live anytime via our Order Tracking Page.
