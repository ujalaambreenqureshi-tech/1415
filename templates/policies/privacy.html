Privacy & Security Policy

Safeguarding customer privacy and payment data integrity

1. Strict Payment Information Protection

Jumma Couture By Noman complies with international PCI-DSS standards. We never collect, store, or view full credit or debit card numbers, CVVs, or bank login credentials on our servers. All card transactions are tokenized via 3D Secure 2.0.

2. Personal Data & Shopify Synchronization

Personal details (name, delivery address, email, and contact number) are utilized solely to process your made-to-order garments, calculate shipping rates, and transmit order updates through official Shopify integrations.

3. No Third-Party Reselling

We never monetize, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers or marketing lists.
