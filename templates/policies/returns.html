Return & Refund Policy

Our commitment to artisanal excellence and customer satisfaction

1. 7-Day Inspection Window

Because every garment is crafted with authentic pure silks and hand-embroidery, patrons may request an exchange or return within 7 calendar days of physical delivery if the item is unworn, in original condition with security tags intact.

2. Refund Execution

Approved refunds are credited directly back to the original payment source (Bank Transfer, Easypaisa, or JazzCash) within 5 to 7 business days following inspection.

3. Custom Sizing & Bespoke Orders

Garments made to bespoke customer measurements or custom-dyed shades are final sale unless a manufacturing defect is present.
