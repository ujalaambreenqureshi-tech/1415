Worldwide Shipping Policy

Express door-to-door delivery across Pakistan, GCC, North America & Europe

1. Payment & Fulfillment Requirement

All shipments are initiated exclusively upon payment verification via Bank Transfer, Easypaisa, or JazzCash. Cash on Delivery (COD) is not offered by our business.

2. Delivery Timelines

  • Pakistan Domestic Express: 2 to 4 business days. Flat fee Rs. 500.
  • United Arab Emirates & Saudi Arabia (GCC): 3 to 5 business days via DHL Express. Flat fee Rs. 4,500.
  • United Kingdom, USA, Canada, Australia & Europe: 4 to 7 business days via DHL/FedEx Priority. Flat fee Rs. 7,500.

3. Tracking & Transit Insurance

Every international order is dispatched with end-to-end tracking and comprehensive transit insurance covering precious hand-embroidered silks and jewelry.
